## Problem Statement

We chose **"Break the Barriers"** — take something powerful locked behind expertise, cost, language, or infrastructure and put it in everyone's hands.

Professional fact-checking used to require journalists, days of research, and institutional resources. ForwardCheck-AI puts that investigative power in anyone's pocket through a Telegram bot.
