# ForwardCheck-AI — Submission Summary (V0)

## Summary (197 words)

Every day, millions of people forward viral messages without knowing if they're true. By the time a fact-checker responds, the damage is done.

ForwardCheck-AI is a Telegram bot that fact-checks any forwarded message in under 60 seconds. Forward a suspicious claim, and six AI agents go to work — like a tiny investigative newsroom in your pocket.

Here's how it works: A Classifier (Haiku) routes the message. A Strategist (Opus 4.6) plans the investigation. Two to three Investigators (Sonnet) dig into sources in parallel. A Devil's Advocate (Opus 4.6) tries to tear the findings apart. And a Judge (Opus 4.6) delivers the final verdict — with visible reasoning you can read yourself.

What makes this different: the bot doesn't just say "true" or "false." It shows you *how* the message tries to manipulate you — emotional framing, missing context, cherry-picked data — so you learn to spot it next time.

Three Opus 4.6 agents each showcase a different reasoning mode: strategic planning, adversarial red-teaming, and tool-augmented verification with transparent thinking. AI fighting misinformation at the speed of a forward button.
