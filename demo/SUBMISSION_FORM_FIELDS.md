
## Written Summary (100-200 words)
```
Your mom just forwarded you a message. "NASA confirmed 15 days of total darkness." You know it's fake. But can you prove it before she sends it to 200 more people?

Forward that message to our Telegram bot. Six AI agents investigate it — and you watch them think.

An Opus 4.6 Strategist plans the investigation — not "search Google," but what evidence would prove the claim true or false. Three Sonnet agents search the web in parallel. Then the Devil's Advocate — Opus 4.6 at maximum reasoning effort — tries to tear the findings apart. When it fails, that failure IS the confidence signal. A stress test it couldn't beat.

You see Opus's actual thinking blocks. Confidence breaks down into four auditable scores: evidence strength, source reliability, claim complexity, and counter-argument resilience. No black box.

Three model tiers (Haiku routing, Sonnet search, Opus reasoning). ~$1-2 per investigation. 5,400 lines of TypeScript. Open source.

The forward button gave everyone the power to spread a lie. ForwardCheck-AI gives everyone the power to check one.
```
