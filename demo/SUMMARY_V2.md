Forward a suspicious message to our Telegram bot. In 60 seconds, six AI agents — powered by Opus 4.6's extended thinking — investigate it while you watch their reasoning live.

Before any search fires, an Opus 4.6 Strategist plans the investigation — defining what evidence would prove the claim true or false. Three Sonnet agents search the web in parallel, guided by that plan. Then the Devil's Advocate — Opus 4.6 at maximum reasoning effort — tries to tear the findings apart. When it fails to construct a viable counter-argument, that failure IS the confidence signal. Not a self-reported number. A stress test it couldn't beat.

You see Opus's actual thinking blocks — the real reasoning, not summaries. Confidence is decomposed into four auditable components: evidence strength, source reliability, claim complexity, and counter-argument resilience. No black box.

Three model tiers (Haiku routing, Sonnet search, Opus reasoning). $0.50-2.00 per investigation. 5,400 lines of TypeScript. Open source.
