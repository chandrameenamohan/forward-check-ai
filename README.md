## Judging Criteria (Know What Judges Care About)

| Criteria | Weight | What They're Looking For |
|----------|--------|------------------------|
| **Demo** | **30%** | Working, impressive demo. Holds up live. Genuinely cool to watch. |
| **Impact** | **25%** | Real-world potential. Who benefits? Could this actually become something people use? Fits a problem statement? |
| **Opus 4.6 Use** | **25%** | Creative use of Opus 4.6. Beyond basic integration. Surface surprising capabilities. |
| **Depth & Execution** | **20%** | Pushed past first idea. Sound engineering. Thoughtfully refined. Real craft, not a quick hack. |

### Key Takeaways from Judging Criteria:
- **Demo is king (30%)** — Invest heavily in making the demo polished and impressive
- **Show off Opus 4.6 creatively (25%)** — Don't just use it as a basic LLM call. Show surprising/creative capabilities
- **Impact matters (25%)** — Must solve a real problem for real people
- **Depth over breadth (20%)** — Better to do one thing deeply than many things shallow

## Problem Statements (Pick One)

### 1. Build a Tool That Should Exist
Create the AI-native app or workflow you wish someone had already built. Eliminate busywork. Make hard things effortless.

**Example projects (what "impact" looks like to judges):**
- Contract Lifecycle Autopilot — Extracts obligations from contracts, tracks deadlines with auto reminders
- Product Changelog Publisher — Turns release notes into customer-facing announcements across channels
- Bug Report Enricher — Auto-adds system logs, user history, reproduction steps to support tickets

### 2. Break the Barriers
Take something powerful locked behind expertise, cost, language, or infrastructure and put it in everyone's hands.

**Example projects:**
- Crop Doctor — Image analysis + weather + soil data to diagnose plant diseases and recommend treatments
- Accessibility Auditor — Evaluates websites/documents against accessibility standards, generates remediation plans
- Open Source Hardware Guide — Helps makers with component selection, PCB design, manufacturing

### 3. Amplify Human Judgment
Build AI that makes professionals dramatically more capable without taking them out of the loop.

**Example projects:**
- Brand Safety Monitor — Reviews ad placements, flags reputation risks for marketing teams
- Discovery Anomaly Detector — Flags missing documents in legal discovery based on references
- Grading Calibration Partner — Highlights scoring inconsistencies without overriding instructor judgment
