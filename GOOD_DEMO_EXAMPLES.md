# ForwardCheck-AI — Demo Examples

> Curated examples for the 3-minute hackathon demo video.
> Organized by impact and what each example showcases.

---

## Tier 1 — Lead with these (max wow factor)

### 1. The "Government Money" Forward (Classic WhatsApp scam)

> "BREAKING: RBI announces Rs 5000 direct transfer to all citizens under PM Digital India scheme. Register before Feb 20 using this link: bit.ly/pm-money-2026. Share with family!"

**Why:** Opens the README. Instantly relatable to billions of people. Shows scam detection, urgency tactics, authority impersonation. Verdict: **LIKELY FALSE — Scam**

---

### 2. The Partially-True Manipulation (most impressive verdict)

> "NASA confirmed that Earth will experience 15 days of complete darkness in November 2026 due to a rare planetary alignment between Venus and Jupiter."

**Why:** There IS a real Venus-Jupiter conjunction — but the "15 days of darkness" is fabricated. Shows the system distinguishing the true kernel from the false claim. Triggers **PARTIALLY TRUE — Exaggerated**. Shows Opus 4.6 nuance.

---

### 3. The Emotional Health Forward (real-world harm)

> "EXPOSED: Exposed exposed exposed the new COVID booster contains graphene oxide microchips. 3 German scientists confirmed this. Big pharma is hiding this. DON'T GET VACCINATED. Forward to save lives!!!"

**Why:** Shows manipulation technique detection (emotional framing, false authority, urgency). Devil's Advocate tries to steelman it and fails hard. **LIKELY FALSE — Fabricated**. Demonstrates real harm prevention.

---

## Tier 2 — Show range & depth

### 4. The Out-of-Context Quote (triggers Deep Reasoning)

> "Elon Musk just said 'AI will replace all programmers within 2 years and there's nothing anyone can do about it.' He's already firing all Tesla engineers."

**Why:** Musk HAS made AI-replaces-jobs statements (investigators will find real quotes) but the specific claim is fabricated/distorted. Investigators will disagree → **Deep Reasoning Mode** activates. Shows the confidence spread > 30 trigger. **PARTIALLY TRUE — Out of Context**

---

### 5. The Actually-True Claim (proves the system isn't biased)

> "India became the first country to land a spacecraft near the Moon's south pole in August 2023 with Chandrayaan-3."

**Why:** Critical for credibility — shows the system can say **LIKELY TRUE (94%)**. Devil's Advocate tries to challenge and fails. Judges see it's not a "debunking-only" tool.

---

### 6. The Satire Misread

> "The Onion reports: Congress passes bill requiring all citizens to solve a CAPTCHA before voting in 2026 elections"

**Why:** Shows classifier catching satire instantly — no full investigation needed. Fast, cheap ($0.01). Demonstrates the routing tier.

## Tier 3 — Bonus if time permits

### 7. Financial Misinformation

> "Warren Buffett just sold ALL his Apple stock and is moving everything into Bitcoin. Berkshire Hathaway filing confirms this. Buy BTC now before it hits $500K!"

**Why:** Buffett DID reduce Apple holdings (true kernel), but "ALL" and "moving to Bitcoin" is fabricated. Shows **financial harm prevention**.

---

### 8. The Recirculated Old News

> "JUST IN: Massive earthquake hits Turkey, thousands feared dead. Please pray and share!"

**Why:** Real event from 2023, recirculated as "breaking." Shows **recirculated** nuance tag. Pattern matcher catches the date mismatch.

## Demo Script Recommendation (3 minutes)

| Time | Example | What it shows |
|------|---------|---------------|
| 0:00-0:15 | Intro + forward the scam (#1) | Hook — everyone knows this forward |
| 0:15-0:45 | Show verdict arriving in Telegram | Speed, manipulation analysis |
| 0:45-1:15 | Forward the partial-truth (#2) | Nuance — not just true/false |
| 1:15-1:45 | Show web verdict page with DA reasoning | Transparency, Opus 4.6 thinking |
| 1:45-2:15 | Forward the true claim (#5) | Credibility — system isn't biased |
| 2:15-2:45 | Show deep reasoning trigger (#4) | Technical depth, confidence decomposition |
| 2:45-3:00 | Architecture overview + close | 6 agents, 3-tier models |

**Key insight:** Don't just show false claims. Showing a true claim verified and a partial truth with nuance is more impressive than 3 "LIKELY FALSE" results back to back.
