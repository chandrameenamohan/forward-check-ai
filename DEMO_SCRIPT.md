# ForwardCheck-AI — 3-Minute Demo Script

**Style:** Cinematic. Deliberate. Every sentence earns the next one.
**Tone:** Quiet confidence. Never desperate. Never rushing. The product speaks.
**Pacing:** Slow open → Build tension → Release with the product → Escalate with showcase → Close with conviction.

---

## PRE-PRODUCTION NOTES

**Screen recording setup:**
- Telegram Desktop (dark mode) on the left half
- Web verdict page on the right half (revealed at 1:40)
- Clean desktop. No tabs. No notifications. Nothing exists except this.
- Record at 1080p minimum. 4K if possible.

**Voice recording:**
- Record voiceover separately (not screen audio)
- Speak slower than feels natural. Pauses are your friend.
- A quiet room. No music during recording — add it in post.

**Music suggestion:**
- Something minimal and building. Piano or ambient synth.
- Quiet under voice, swells during transitions.
- Cuts to silence at 2:25 for the thinking reveal.

**Editing:**
- Speed up wait times (investigator searches) to ~3-5 seconds each
- Let the verdict moment land — hold on it for 2 full seconds before speaking
- Use subtle zoom on key UI elements as you narrate them

## THE SCRIPT

---

### ACT I — THE LIE [0:00 – 0:35]

**[VISUAL: Black screen. Text fades in, white on black:]**

> *"A lie gets halfway around the world before the truth has a chance to get its pants on."*
> *— attributed to Mark Twain, Churchill, and Jonathan Swift.*
> *No one actually knows who said it.*
> *Which is kind of the point.*

**[Hold 4 seconds. Fade to black.]**

**[VISUAL: A phone screen. WhatsApp. A forwarded message lands. The familiar double arrow icon. The message reads something like: "BREAKING: Modi government announces Rs 5000 direct transfer to all citizens. Forward to 10 people to claim." It has been forwarded many times.]**

**VOICEOVER:**

"This message has been forwarded six hundred thousand times.

It takes four seconds to forward. It takes a journalist four days to fact-check.

By then — six hundred thousand people have already believed it. Already shared it. Already told their mother.

*(beat)*

We built something that does it in sixty seconds."

**[VISUAL: Cut to Telegram Desktop. Clean. The bot chat is open: @ForwardCheckAI_bot]**

---

### ACT II — THE NEWSROOM [0:35 – 1:40]

**VOICEOVER:**

"This is ForwardCheck AI. You forward it a message. Behind it — six AI agents run an investigation. Not one model answering a question. A *newsroom*."

**[ACTION: Paste/forward the Modi Rs 5000 claim into the Telegram bot. Send it.]**

**[VISUAL: Bot responds: "Investigating your claim..." then status updates begin appearing]**

**VOICEOVER:**

"First — a desk editor reads the message and routes it. Is this a claim? An opinion? A scam? Takes half a second.

*(status update: "Planning investigation...")*

Then — and this is where it gets interesting — an Opus 4.6 strategist *plans the investigation*. Not 'search Google and summarize.' It asks: what specific evidence would prove this true? What would prove it false? It writes the falsification criteria *before anyone searches anything*.

*(status update: "Searching sources..." → "Analyzing domain expertise...")*

Two specialist investigators fan out in parallel. One traces the claim's origin — who published it first, has it been debunked before. The other checks authoritative domain sources — government press releases, official records.

*(status update: "Challenging findings...")*

Now. The part no other system does.

A Devil's Advocate receives every finding. And its only job... is to prove the investigation *wrong*. Opus 4.6 extended thinking. Maximum effort. It constructs the strongest possible counter-argument against what the investigators found."

**[VISUAL: Status update: "Rendering verdict..."]**

**VOICEOVER:**

"And finally — a Judge. Also Opus 4.6. Also thinking at maximum depth. It weighs the evidence, considers the Devil's Advocate's challenge, and if something is contested — it runs its own independent search to verify."

**[VISUAL: The verdict arrives in Telegram. RED badge. "LIKELY FALSE — 8% confidence." A brief summary. A button: "View Full Analysis."]**

*(Hold on this for 2 seconds. Let it land.)*

**VOICEOVER:**

"Eight percent. Likely false.

But that number alone isn't the point."

---

### ACT III — THE REVEAL [1:40 – 2:35]

**[ACTION: Click "View Full Analysis." The web verdict page loads.]**

**VOICEOVER:**

"Most fact-checkers tell you *what* to think. We show you *how* to think."

**[VISUAL: Scroll to Manipulation Techniques section]**

"This message uses authority impersonation — it claims to be an official government announcement. It uses urgency tactics — 'forward immediately or lose your chance.' And emotional manipulation — financial hope designed to bypass your critical thinking.

ForwardCheck doesn't just label a message false. It teaches you to see the machinery of a lie."

**[VISUAL: Scroll to "What Would Prove This Wrong" section]**

"This section comes directly from the Opus 4.6 Strategist — the falsification criteria it wrote before anyone searched anything. 'An official government gazette announcing this scheme in 2024.' That's what would change the verdict. We looked. It doesn't exist."

**[VISUAL: Scroll to Devil's Advocate thinking excerpt. Slow zoom.]**

*(Music drops to silence.)*

**VOICEOVER (slower now):**

"And here... is the moment.

This is Opus 4.6's actual thinking. The Devil's Advocate — trying to argue that the investigators got it wrong. Read it."

**[VISUAL: Hold on the thinking excerpt for 4-5 seconds. Let the audience read. Something like: "I attempted to find any credible source supporting this claim. The government's official press release archive contains no such announcement. The closest match is a debunked 2019 scheme recycled with a new date..."]**

**VOICEOVER:**

"It tried. It failed. 'Counter-argument failed.'

That failure is our highest confidence signal. Not because a model said 'I'm 92% sure.' Because a model genuinely *tried to prove itself wrong* — and couldn't.

That's a fundamentally different kind of confidence."

**[VISUAL: Scroll to Confidence Decomposition — the four-component breakdown]**

"And we don't hide behind a single number. Evidence strength: 92. Source reliability: 88. Claim complexity: 95 — this was easy to verify. Counter-argument resilience: 96. Every component visible. Every component auditable."

---

### ACT IV — THE CLOSE [2:35 – 3:00]

**[VISUAL: Quick cut — forward a SECOND claim. A health claim: "WHO declares green tea cures cancer." Fast-forward the investigation. Verdict arrives: PARTIALLY TRUE — 45%, nuance tag: EXAGGERATED]**

**VOICEOVER:**

"And it handles nuance. This health claim isn't false — a real study found a compound 'possibly' relevant. But the viral message turned 'possibly' into 'definitely.' ForwardCheck catches the distortion. Partially true. Exaggerated."

**[VISUAL: Return to black screen. Text fades in:]**

> **ForwardCheck AI**
> *Six agents. Three tiers. Sixty seconds.*

**VOICEOVER:**

"We use Opus 4.6 three times per investigation. Each time doing something different. Planning. Challenging. Judging. We use Sonnet for the searches, Haiku for routing — because the right model for the right job isn't just cost-efficient. It's honest engineering.

*(beat)*

A lie gets halfway around the world in four seconds.

We just gave truth a sixty-second head start.

ForwardCheck AI. Built with Opus 4.6."

**[VISUAL: Hold logo + bot link for 3 seconds. Fade to black.]**
