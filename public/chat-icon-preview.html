Rationale
Design Decisions
Shape: Shield-shaped chat bubble — the angular peak at the top evokes a heraldic shield (trust/authority), while the sharp tail at bottom-left reads as "chat". The miter joins keep edges razor-sharp.

Checkmark: Bold geometric verification mark centered in the body. Two strokes, round caps — instantly recognizable even at 16px.

Color: Uses the existing ForwardCheck accent green (#22c55e) on the app's dark background (#0a0a0f). Maintains visual consistency with the existing shield favicon.

Sizes: Favicon-optimized variant uses 1.8x thicker strokes (36/48 vs 20/32) to maintain legibility at 16-32px. Standard variant used at 48px and above for refined proportions.

Concept: "Shield Chat" — a shield that IS a chat bubble. Investigation + conversation + verification in a single glyph. Forensic, not casual.
